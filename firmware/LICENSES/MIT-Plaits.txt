Mutable Instruments' eurorack code by Emilie Gillet <https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack>, ported to
fixed-point C by Hortator (DEADACTIVE, <https://github.com/DeadActive/hortator>) and carried into Melodee Custom's
DRM engine (2026). The ported parts stay under the MIT licence below:
