# Melodee Custom 1.2.7

Two colour themes: LOOM A and LOOM B.

- **Install:** [web installer](https://theidiotwizard.github.io/melodee_custom/webapp/installer/)
  (Chrome or Edge, USB).
- **Edit:** [web editor](https://theidiotwizard.github.io/melodee_custom/webapp/editor/)

## LOOM A and LOOM B

HOME menu (hold HOME) → **COLOR** now offers **LOOM A** and **LOOM B** next to **MONO**. Both keep MONO's black
background and white text, and give each track a colour from [LOOM-8](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-loom)'s
default theme by Charles Vestal: LOOM A its tracks 1–4's, LOOM B its tracks 5–8's.

| Track | LOOM A | LOOM B |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | pink | orange |
| 2 | teal | green |
| 3 | yellow | purple |
| 4 | blue | tan |

The track chips, lanes, mixer and step grids show each track's colour. A track's pages (values, curves, the
selection) take its colour, and their four knobs get shades of it. MIDI tracks 5–8 reuse colours 1–4.

MONO stays the default; the choice is remembered.

## Upgrading

Install as usual; projects, presets, settings and backups are kept. As before, this was tested on the computer build
of the firmware, not yet on an FM-1.
