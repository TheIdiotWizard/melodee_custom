# Melodee Custom 1.3.8

## FILTER: LP and HP are MONO2K's chain

The FILTER page's MODE is now OFF, LP or HP, both MONO2K's chain (1.3.7's M2K mode): DIST into a resonant filter
that clips at the machine's ceiling, the cutoff following the note. LP: CUT is MONO2K's WDTH, RES its LPQ. HP (new):
CUT is MONO2K's BASE, RES its HPQ, the same DIST. TRIO's BP and NOT are gone. CUT now shows steps, not Hz.

Projects and sounds of 1.3.7 or before load with BP and HP as HP, NOT and M2K as LP. The factory presets of DUALVCO,
ANALOG4 and FMTONE that used TRIO's filter have their cutoffs moved onto the new scale.

## FMTONE: all of MONO2K's FM sounds

FMTONE has 24 presets: Mini-Tone's BRASS, PLUCK LEAD, RESO PLUCK and FIFTH RING, and all 17 of MONO2K's library FM
sounds (EPIANO, FMBELL, DXBASS, GLASPAD, GROWL, GLASSKY, FMPAD, DRONE, BUBBLES, ZAP, FMKICK, FMSNARE, FMHAT, COWBELL,
CONGA, WOODBLK, TING) and its three FM INITs (FM KEYS, FM GONG, FM BASS), refitted through the new filter with their
DIST and level. GROWL is no longer quiet. Stars work on all 24.

Everything from [1.3.7](RELEASE_1.3.7.md) is included.
