# Melodee Custom 1.3.7

MONO2K's bite: its track chain on the FILTER page, and its SAW and PULS sounds through it.

## FILTER: MONO2K's chain (M2K)

The FILTER page has a new MODE, **M2K**: MONO2K's track chain, ported. DIST drives into a resonant low-pass that
clips at the machine's ceiling (the scream), then MONO2K's high-pass and smoothing poles; the cutoffs follow the
note. CUT is MONO2K's WDTH, RES its LPQ, and the page's fourth knob is now **DIST** (0 = MONO2K's 64; ENV › FLT
stays on ENV DEST). It works on every FILTER engine: SAW, PULS, ENS, WAV, FMTONE, DUALVCO, ANALOG4.

SAW and PULS now carry MONO2K's own library sounds through it: SAW ACID, REESE, MOOG, SUPERLD, BRASS, STRINGS and
SUPERSAW (its SAW INIT); PULS PWMBASS, SQUARE, PWMPAD and REED (its PULS INIT). Their envelopes were fitted to
MONO2K's renders. The power-on SAW sound is SUPERSAW.

Everything from [1.3.6](RELEASE_1.3.6.md) is included.
